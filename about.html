💛 Our story

Why we started Joy of Writing

A writer, a room full of children's ideas, and one stubborn belief — that a child's own voice is worth protecting.

Anuradha

Founder & Writing Coach
20+ years a writer

Hello, I'm Anuradha

I have been a professional writer for the last two decades. Children and writing are two of the things I love most in this world — and Joy of Writing is my attempt to bring the two together.

Somewhere along the way I noticed something. Children still have wild, brilliant, completely unrepeatable ideas — but fewer and fewer places to put them. Screens got faster. Attention got shorter. And now machines will happily write anything for anyone, in seconds.

I don't think that makes writing less valuable. I think it makes a child's own voice more precious than ever.

So this is what we do here: give children a reason to pick up a pen, a real person to read what they wrote, and honest, kind feedback on what shone and what could be even better. No grades. No red pen. Just encouragement, a little craft, and the quiet thrill of finishing something entirely your own.

If your child has a story in them, I would love to read it. 💛
