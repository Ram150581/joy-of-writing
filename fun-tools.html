Four things worth knowing before you start

🗺️ The shape of a story

Your seeds tell you what to write about. This tells you how to build it. Follow the path — or wander off it, that's allowed too!

🌍
First

Show us where we are

  • What can you see, hear and smell in this place?
  • Is it hot? Noisy? Empty? Scary at night?

✨ Two or three sentences is plenty. Don't describe every single thing!

👋
Next

Introduce your people

  • What does your hero look like? What are they wearing?
  • One thing they always do or say — a habit, a favourite word.
  • What do they want more than anything in the world?

✨ Show it happening instead of listing it. "She checked her pockets three times" tells us more than "she was nervous".

💥
Then

Something goes wrong

  • What breaks the ordinary day?
  • Who — or what — is standing in the way?
  • How does your hero feel the moment it happens?

✨ This is where your story really starts. Get here quickly!

🌀
In the middle

Try, fail, try again

  • What's the first thing they try? Let it go wrong!
  • Now make things a little worse before they get better.
  • Who helps? Who makes it harder?

✨ A story where everything works first time is a story with no story in it.

✨
At the end

Give us a twist

  • Surprise us — did the help come from somewhere nobody expected?
  • Was the problem something else all along?
  • What has changed for your hero now?

✨ Finish on a picture, not an explanation. Let us see the last moment.

You don't have to use all five. Even three of these makes a proper story. 💛
