What happens in the 30 minutes

  • A warm-up game — everyone writes one silly sentence. No wrong answers, and it breaks the ice.
  • One real story — Anuradha gives a spark, and the children write by hand for ten minutes.
  • Reading out, if they'd like to — nobody is made to. Shy writers can just listen.
What you need: paper, a pen, and a quiet corner. That's genuinely it — no printing, no apps to install, no preparation.

Questions parents ask

Does my child have to be on camera?

No. Cameras and microphones are entirely optional — plenty of children prefer to listen and write with theirs off, and that's completely fine.

Is this really free?

Yes. No payment details, no trial that starts charging. If you'd like to carry on afterwards we have paid plans, but nobody will be pushed.

Do I need to sit with my child?

No. Children from 6 upwards can join on their own — settle them in with paper and a pen, and leave them to it. You're very welcome to stay if your child would rather you did.

What if we miss it?

Register anyway — we'll email you when the next one is scheduled.
