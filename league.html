How it works

  • Choose a story starter — there is a set to pick from, so everyone begins with an idea they like.
  • Write your story — one story, by hand, in your own words.
  • Prizes to be won — with a separate contest for each age group.
What to bring: just yourself. Stationery (pen or pencil, and paper) is provided.

Questions

Who can participate?

Anyone aged 6 or above. There are three age groups: 6 to 10, 11 to 15, and above 15 and adults. Parents are welcome to participate in the adults group.

How do I pay the ₹250?

By UPI, straight to Anuradha. After you register, the page and your confirmation email show how.

Do I need to be a Joy of Writing student?

No. The contest is open to everyone.

What is a story starter?

A first idea for a story, such as a character, a place or a surprising moment. You choose the one you like and carry it wherever you want.

Who is running it?

Anuradha of Joy of Writing, who has been a professional writer for two decades. More about her →
