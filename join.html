Questions parents ask

Can we stop whenever we like?

Yes. There is no lock-in and no notice period — stop at the end of any month, no reason needed. Just tell us, or simply don't renew.

What if we miss a live session?

Nothing is lost. Sessions run through the month and your child joins the ones that suit you. The writing and feedback carry on regardless.

Does my child have to be on camera?

No. Cameras and microphones are entirely optional — plenty of children prefer to listen and write with theirs off.

My child is 5, or 16. Can they still join?

The sessions are grouped by age, from 6 to 15. A little either side is usually fine — write to Anuradha and she'll tell you honestly whether it will suit.

Who is teaching?

Anuradha, a professional writer for two decades. She reads every story herself — that is the whole point of this. More about her →
